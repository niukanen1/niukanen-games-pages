Last updated 28 September 2026

Your game progress is stored on this device. If you are signed in to Game Center, Lamplight receives your player name and ID to show that you are connected. If ads are enabled in your version, Google may process data to show and measure non-personalized ads.

What is stored on the device

So that you can pick up where you left off, the app stores on the device:

  • the rooms you have lit or skipped, stars, best times and move counts;
  • rooms in progress, together with the moves you can undo;
  • your Daily Room streak, candles and hint supply;
  • settings (theme, haptics, showing the timer) and which tips have already been shown;
  • the date of the first launch and the number of launches, so ads do not bother new players.

Lamplight does not send game progress to the developer, Game Center, or Google. Like the data of any app, it may be included in your iPhone backup if you have backups turned on.

Game Center

Lamplight contacts Game Center at launch. If you are signed in, the app receives your display name and player identifier to show the connection status. This data is not added to your game progress.

Switching or disconnecting a Game Center account does not erase progress on the device. Lamplight does not currently synchronize saves through Game Center or iCloud.

How to delete your data

“Reset progress” in Settings erases your rooms, stars, streak and hints. If Settings shows “Ad privacy settings”, you can use it to review or change your ad choices. Your Game Center account is managed in iOS Settings. Deleting the app removes its local data from the device.

What the app does not do

  • it does not create its own account and does not receive your e-mail address or phone number;
  • it does not request access to your contacts or photos;
  • Lamplight does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. When advertising is enabled, it requests non-personalized ads and disables Google’s publisher first-party ID. Google may still process device or app identifiers and advertising, interaction and diagnostic data as described below.
  • it does not send your game board or progress to the advertising partner: the Daily Room is generated directly on the device.

Ads and purchases

If ads are enabled in your version of Lamplight, the app uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s UMP consent platform. The app requests only non-personalized ads and starts loading them only after UMP permits ad requests. If ads are not enabled, Google Mobile Ads and the consent form do not start, and no ad requests are sent.

Google may process the IP address to estimate general location, device or app identifiers, information about ads shown and interactions with them, and crash and performance data. This data is used to display ads, measure their performance, and diagnose problems.

For more about data processing and controls, see the Google Privacy Policy, My Ad Center, and Apple Privacy Policy.

Purchases are not currently offered. If they become available, Apple will process payments and purchase history through the App Store; Lamplight will accept only purchases verified by Apple.

Children

Lamplight does not ask for age and is not designed specifically for children. Parents should consider the Game Center and Google Mobile Ads data processing described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new revision with a new date will appear in the app and at the privacy policy link on Lamplight's App Store page.

This website

This website has no analytics, advertising cookies, forms or payments. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information, such as IP addresses and access logs, to deliver and protect the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts are hosted with this site.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request and resolve support issues. Ask us by email if you want your support emails deleted, subject to any retention obligations.
