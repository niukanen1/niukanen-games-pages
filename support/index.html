Help with Lamplight

Support

Stuck on a room, spotted a bug, or have something to say about Lamplight? Write to me.

niukanen.games@gmail.com

Helpful to include

  • Your iPhone model and iOS version.
  • The game version, if you know it.
  • The house and room number, if it’s about a puzzle.
  • What happened and what you expected.
  • A screenshot, if it helps. Please leave out passwords and other private details.

Common questions

Is Lamplight available yet?

Lamplight is preparing for its iPhone release. This site will link to the App Store once it’s available.

Which languages does it support?

English and Russian. You can change the language in the game’s settings.

Is there a time limit?

No. Rooms have no countdown or time limit. You can turn the optional elapsed-time display off in Settings.

How do I delete local progress?

Use “Reset progress” in Settings. This deletes the game progress stored on this iPhone. Deleting the app also removes its local data. Read the Privacy Policy for information about Apple services and advertising partners.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Not at the moment. Progress is saved on the iPhone you play on. Signing in to Game Center shows that you’re connected, but it doesn’t move your saves to another device.

Where’s the privacy policy?

Read it here, in English or Russian.
